Bio

Hyuncheol Yoon, Ed.S., is a doctoral student in Counseling and Counselor Education at the University of Florida. He holds an Education Specialist (Ed.S.) degree in Counselor Education from the University of Florida and two master’s degrees, one from Yonsei University and another from the University of Florida. He is also a licensed school counselor. Hyuncheol’s research focuses on school counselor professional identity, counselor-to-student ratios, school-family partnerships, the use of technology in school counseling, and student and teacher well-being.
